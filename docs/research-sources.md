### Theme evidence

1. Software-defined & 5G networks: Joyce's open-source 5G core benchmarking (10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3441725), virtualised network functions, 5G testbeds, and software-based networking.
2. Intelligent network management: Joyce's review of intelligent end-to-end slicing (10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3516051); Daniel's ORCID lists multi-agent reinforcement learning for resource allocation in terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks, and slice admission control.
3. IoT & connected sensing: Daniel's machine-learning virtual sensor work (10.3390/jsan9020025); Joyce's digital health testbeds and connected applications.
4. Secure cyber-physical systems: Daniel's AI-driven intrusion detection (10.3390/ijerph19095367), and ORCID publications on water distribution systems and microgrids.
